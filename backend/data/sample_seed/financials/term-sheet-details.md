# Contoso AI — Inbound term sheet (Apr 2026)

## Headline
- **Lead investor**: undisclosed US-based growth fund (Tier-1, prior portfolio includes 3 dev-tools companies that IPO'd).
- **Amount**: $30M primary.
- **Pre-money valuation**: $220M (flat from Series A round in Aug 2024).
- **Post-money**: $250M.
- **Round name**: Series B.

## Economic terms
- **Liquidation preference**: 2x **participating** preferred (NB: this is the contentious term — Series A was 1x non-participating).
- **Dividend**: 6% non-cumulative.
- **Anti-dilution**: broad-based weighted average.
- **Pro-rata rights**: yes, on subsequent rounds for all existing investors.

## Governance
- **Board**: +1 from investor (5-seat board: 2 founders, 1 Series A, 1 Series B, 1 independent).
- **Protective provisions**: standard NVCA, with addition of veto over (a) any acquisition < $200M, (b) any change to data-residency or AI-safety policy disclosed in the data room.

## Diligence focus areas (raised by lead's tech DD)
1. **Customer concentration**: top 3 logos = 26% of ARR. They want concentration below 20% by close.
2. **Model dependency**: 78% of agent actions depend on third-party foundation models. They want a fallback plan and at least 2 redundant providers per agent.
3. **SOC 2 Type II**: must be issued before close.

## Counterpoints from CFO memo
- 2x participating effectively caps founder/early-employee returns at acquisition prices < $400M. With ARR trajectory we expect $80M+ ARR by 2028 at ~10x = $800M, so participating only matters in downside cases. Still — Legal/Board flagged this as misaligned signal.
- We have a competing soft offer from a strategic acquirer ($120M cash + $40M earnout) — not preferred outcome but useful negotiation leverage.
