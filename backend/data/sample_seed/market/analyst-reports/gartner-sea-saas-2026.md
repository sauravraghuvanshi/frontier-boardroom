# Gartner — SEA enterprise SaaS 2026 (excerpt, summarized)

Citation: Gartner, "Southeast Asia Enterprise SaaS Forecast 2026," published Mar 2026.

## Top-line forecasts
- SEA mid-market SaaS spend: **+24% YoY** (2026 over 2025), reaching $32B aggregate.
- Developer productivity / AI-agent tooling subsegment: **+47% YoY**, fastest-growing line item.
- Singapore-headquartered SaaS vendors win **38%** of cross-border ASEAN deals — premium for trusted-jurisdiction billing entity.

## Top buyer concerns (SEA, n=412)
1. Data residency (cited by 71% of regulated-industry buyers).
2. Vendor longevity for AI agents (47% — buyers worried about consolidation).
3. Pricing transparency (42% — frustration with usage-based pricing models).
4. Foundation-model independence (38% — concern about LLM provider lock-in).

## Implications for Contoso AI
- Validates urgency of Jakarta + Vietnam residency build.
- Validates Contoso's per-seat pricing model (vs Cursor's usage-based) as a market preference.
- Foundation-model-neutrality argument resonates more in SEA than India.

## Gartner Magic Quadrant for AI engineering agents — SEA
- Leaders quadrant 2026: Acme Devbots (visionary axis), Cursor (execution axis).
- Challengers: Contoso AI (named).
- Niche players: BetaCode, GammaShip, Cohort.dev.
- **Contoso AI ranked 6th overall**, 4th in India sub-segment.
