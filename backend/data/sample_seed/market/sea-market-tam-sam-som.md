# Market sizing — AI agents for engineering teams in SEA (2026)

Source: Gartner SEA SaaS 2026, IDC India Cloud 2026, internal bottom-up build from Glassdoor + LinkedIn dev-count.

## Definition
"AI agents for engineering teams" = software products that perform autonomous or semi-autonomous engineering tasks (code review, PR triage, test generation, on-call response, infra ops) on behalf of human developers, priced per dev seat or per agent action.

## TAM / SAM / SOM
- **TAM** (SEA, 2026): **$9.4B** addressable spend by all enterprise engineering orgs in SEA on dev-productivity / AI-agent tooling.
- **SAM** (mid-market + upper SMB, English-capable buyers, eng orgs 50–2000 devs): **$2.1B**.
- **SOM** (year-1 realistic, given Contoso's product fit and SEA brand): **$48M**.

## Why SAM is smaller than TAM/2
- Excludes APAC megacaps (Grab, GoTo, Sea Group) — they build their own dev-productivity tooling.
- Excludes engineering orgs < 50 devs (CAC payback > 24 months at current pricing).
- Excludes regulated SEA financial-services until per-country residency is delivered (Sep 2026 earliest).

## Anchor markets in priority order
1. **Singapore** — English, regulatory clarity, dev-tools brand awareness highest. Aim 6 logos by Q3.
2. **Indonesia (Jakarta only)** — large engineering pools at GoTo-ecosystem ISVs and fintechs. Aim 4 logos.
3. **Thailand (Bangkok)** — Krungsri-bank ecosystem, payments fintech. Aim 2 logos.
4. **Vietnam (HCMC)** — outsourcing-heavy; lower seat ACV but volume.

## Year-1 avoid list
- **Philippines** — long sales cycles, English-good but regulatory fragmentation.
- **Malaysia** — price-sensitive, MYR exposure, lower dev density.

## Bottom-up validation
- ~430K professional developers in SAM (5 SEA countries, eng orgs 50–2000).
- Reachable serviceable subset (English-capable buyers): ~280K.
- Conservative monetization: 12% adoption × $89/seat/mo × 12 = $35.9M ARR (year-3).
- $48M SOM-year-1 includes pilots + partner-channel revenue.
