# Talent availability — SEA (engineering + enterprise sales)

Source: internal recruiter intel + Glassdoor 2025 hiring data + 18 informational interviews with SEA dev-tools founders.

## Singapore
- **Senior platform engineers** (AI agents, infra): high cost, low supply. Median time-to-hire: **3 months**. Median total comp: $180K (engineer L5 equivalent). Competition: Anthropic SG office, Sea (Garena), Stripe, GovTech.
- **Enterprise AEs (dev tools)**: limited pool (~30 candidates actively open). Median OTE: $180K. Top 3 closers come from Atlassian, GitLab, Snowflake SG.
- **Recommendation**: hire 1 senior platform eng + 1 GM via executive search firm; rest of GTM via Hudson Singapore.

## Indonesia (Jakarta)
- **Engineers**: mid cost, growing supply. Strong Bahasa Indonesia + English bilingual pool. Median total comp: $40–60K for senior eng. Competition: GoTo, Tokopedia, Traveloka.
- **AEs**: limited dev-tools background. Plan to hire 1 enterprise AE with broader SaaS background.
- **Recommendation**: open Jakarta hub (WeWork) by Q2 2026 once first design partner signed.

## Vietnam (HCMC)
- **Engineers**: low cost, fast supply. Lower English fluency (avg IELTS 5.5 vs ID 6.5). Median total comp: $25–40K.
- **AEs**: very limited dev-tools background; partner-led GTM is more practical for VN.
- **Recommendation**: hire SDR + SE for VN; cover with SG-based AE for deal closes.

## Cross-cutting
- **Visa & relocation**: EP/S Pass timelines in SG = 2–4 weeks. ID = 8–12 weeks for foreign hires (typically not used). VN = 6–10 weeks. Plan accordingly.
- **Onboarding time-to-quota**: SG AE ramp = 4 months; ID AE = 6 months; VN SDR = 3 months.
