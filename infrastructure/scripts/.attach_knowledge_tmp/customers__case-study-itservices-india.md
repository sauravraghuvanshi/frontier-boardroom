# Customer case study — TopTier IT Services (anonymized)

**Industry**: India IT services (Tier-1, ~280K employees globally).
**Engineering footprint**: ~95K developers in scope for Contoso deployment.
**Contoso AI products**: Code Review Agent only (starter tier, high-volume pricing).
**Region**: India-resident.

## Deal shape
- Bulk seat license: 25,000 dev seats at negotiated rate of **$11/seat/mo** (89% off list starter pricing, justified by volume).
- ARR: 25,000 × $11 × 12 = **$3.3M ARR**.
- 3-year commit signed Mar 2026.
- Largest single Contoso AI deal to date; represents 17% of total ARR.

## Use case
- Code review at PR time across 1,400+ client engagement repos.
- Strict mandate: zero customer source code leaves customer-controlled tenancy; inference performed in Contoso India region under negotiated DPA. Audit logs exported to TopTier's GRC platform daily.

## Results (3 months in)
- ~14% reduction in P1/P2 defects escaping to UAT (measured against pre-Contoso baseline on similar engagements).
- 4 of 1,400 repos onboarded to Test-Gen pilot — too early to read results.

## Risk
- **Concentration risk**: this deal alone makes top-3 logo concentration = 26% of ARR (flagged by lead Series B investor — see `term-sheet-details.md`). Investor wants <20% by close.
- Mitigation: aggressive expansion of mid-market India + SEA logos to dilute concentration.

## Quote
> "We evaluated GitHub Copilot, Cursor, Acme Devbots, and Contoso. Only Contoso could guarantee in-country inference and accept our security architecture as-is. The 89% volume discount didn't hurt either." — CIO office, TopTier IT Services
