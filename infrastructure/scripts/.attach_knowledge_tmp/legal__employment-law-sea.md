# Employment law — hiring in SEA for Contoso AI

Prepared by Trilegal (India counsel) and Allen & Gledhill (SG counsel), Apr 2026.

## Singapore
- **EP/S Pass timelines**: 2–4 weeks for senior roles; faster for SG citizens.
- **Local entity**: not strictly required if employing via EOR (Globalization Partners, Remote.com). EOR adds ~13% to fully-loaded comp.
- **Recommended path**: register Contoso AI Singapore Pte Ltd (3–6 weeks) before headcount > 6; cheaper than long-term EOR.
- **Stock option treatment**: ESOP issued from US parent; SG ESOP plan registration with IRAS straightforward but adds ~$15K legal.

## Indonesia
- **PT PMA required** to hire directly (foreign-owned LLC).
- EOR available for first 2 roles, then PT PMA mandatory.
- **Setup**: 8–12 weeks, $25K legal + $40K minimum paid-up capital.
- **Local director**: Indonesian national required on the PT PMA.
- **Severance**: 2 months/yr of service — material exit cost if hiring doesn't work.

## Vietnam
- **Rep office** permitted for sales-only activities; **LLC required** if employing product/eng staff.
- **LLC setup**: 4–6 months, $20K legal + WHT setup.
- **Foreign work permit**: 6–10 weeks per foreign hire; local Vietnamese hires faster.

## Thailand
- **BOI (Board of Investment) promotion** possible: 15-year corporate tax incentive for tech firms meeting tech-investment threshold.
- **Setup**: 4–8 weeks for BOI application + LLC.
