# Contoso AI — 2025 Q4 P&L Summary

Fiscal period: Oct–Dec 2025. Reporting entities: Contoso AI Pvt Ltd (Delhi, India) and Contoso AI Inc (Delaware, US).

## Headline numbers
- ARR exit: **$18.6M** (+71% YoY)
- Gross margin: **71%** (up from 66% in Q4-2024 as inference unit-cost dropped)
- Monthly net burn: **$1.4M**
- Cash balance (EOM Dec 2025): **$26.6M**
- Implied runway at current burn: **18 months**

## ARR by product line
- Contoso Code Review Agent: 41% of ARR
- Contoso On-Call Agent (PagerDuty/Opsgenie companion): 26%
- Contoso PR Triage + Test-Gen bundle: 22%
- Contoso Infra-Ops Agent (paid pilots only): 7%
- Professional services & onboarding: 4%

## Top drivers
- 6 new India enterprise logos (2 banks, 1 unicorn fintech, 3 IT services firms). ARR per FTE up 18% QoQ.
- Net dollar retention reached **128%** — driven by seat expansion after the Code Review Agent lands (median land = 25 dev seats, median 12-month expansion = 78 seats).
- Inference cost per agent action fell 22% QoQ after Code Review Agent moved off general-purpose models onto a fine-tuned 13B model on dedicated GPU pool in Delhi/NCR.

## Drags
- Cloud spend up because of 2 new India regions (Mumbai + Hyderabad redundancy) added for enterprise customers requiring in-country residency.
- Sales & marketing up 31% QoQ ahead of Q1 2026 SEA GTM acceleration.

## Board metrics
- Rule of 40 = 71% growth − 12% FCF margin loss = **59** (top quartile for Series B AI infra).
- Magic Number Q4: **1.4** — sales efficiency strong, supports continued hiring.
